Algernon Sidney's Scaffold Paper
Dutch and German translations and editions There are at least five different print versions of The Very COPY of PAPER Delivered to the SHERIFFS, Upon the Scaffold on Tower-hill, on Friday Decemb. 7. 1683. By Algernoon Sidney, Esq; Before his Execution there: One in English, two in Dutch and two in German. In the book I am currently writing, I explain how they relate to each other.
